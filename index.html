How I Work

我怎么做事 · four principles, stated briefly
01

Constraints before solutions

The answer depends on real limits, not on industry habit. Setting labels aside and reasoning from the constraints often leads somewhere else.

先问约束，再谈方案。答案取决于真实的限制，而非行业惯例；放下标签、 从约束重新推导，结论常与惯例不同。

02

End to end, by myself

From interface and data to inference and packaging, I go through each layer myself — not to prove breadth, but because crossing every layer is how you find where a problem actually sits.

端到端自己走完。从界面、数据到推理与打包，每一层都自己穿过；不是为了 证明覆盖面，而是只有穿过每一层，才知道问题卡在哪里。

03

Repeatable gates over "looks fine"

Tests, baselines, mutation smoke checks: confirm the gate turns red before trusting its green. Stability comes from repeated triggering, not from a few observations.

可复跑的门禁胜过"看着差不多"。测试、基线、变异冒烟——先确认门禁能 抓到红，再信它给的绿；稳定性来自可重复触发，而非几次观察。

04

Turn what breaks into public knowledge

When a framework misbehaves, I first separate my misuse from a real defect. If it is the latter, I reduce it to a minimal reproduction and report it upstream.

把踩过的坑变成公共财产。遇到框架问题，先分清用法错误还是真实缺陷； 是后者就做成最小复现上报上游。

Issue #7107 · undocumented thread-safety contract

Page.update() thread-safety was undocumented; run_thread() does not return to the UI thread.

Page.update() 线程安全契约未文档化；run_thread() 并不回到 UI 线程。

Issue #7108 → PR #7113 · unsafe cross-thread schedulingVERIFIED

The 1.x scheduler calls asyncio.Event.set() across threads unsafely. Independently reproduced and cross-checked against the fix.

1.x 调度器跨线程调用 asyncio.Event.set() 不安全。 独立复现并对修复结果做了对照验证。

Issue #7120 · uncancellable run_thread worker

run_thread() workers could not be cancelled; the process lingered after the window closed. Minimal reproduction with a clean venv and a no-worker control group.

run_thread() worker 无法取消，关窗后进程滞留。 用干净 venv 的最小复现与无 worker 对照组定位。

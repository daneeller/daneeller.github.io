How I Work

Four principles, stated briefly
01

Constraints before solutions

The answer depends on real limits, not on industry habit. Setting labels aside and reasoning from the constraints often leads somewhere else.

02

End to end, by myself

From interface and data to inference and packaging, I go through each layer myself — not to prove breadth, but because crossing every layer is how you find where a problem actually sits.

03

Repeatable gates over "looks fine"

Tests, baselines, mutation smoke checks: confirm the gate turns red before trusting its green. Stability comes from repeated triggering, not from a few observations.

04

Turn what breaks into public knowledge

When a framework misbehaves, I first separate my misuse from a real defect. If it is the latter, I reduce it to a minimal reproduction and report it upstream.
